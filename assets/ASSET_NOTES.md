# Generated visual assets

## Shanxi image sets

The website uses five AI-generated contact sheets. Each sheet is a 4 × 3 grid of twelve separate scenes; the interface crops one panel at a time with CSS so archive cards and detail screens behave like individual illustrations. The generated source files are:

- illustration-sheets/sheet-01.png — 12 Shanxi village architecture and memory scenes: loess plateau settlements, gray-brick courtyard gates, cave dwellings, stone lanes, opera stage, village well, and evening rooftops.
- illustration-sheets/sheet-02.png — 12 Shanxi dryland and seasonal field scenes: wheat, millet, corn, orchard, terraces, harvest, irrigation and contemporary farming.
- illustration-sheets/sheet-03.png — 12 craft scenes: lacquer work, paper-cutting, inkstone making, embroidery, woodblock printing, carpentry, pottery, basketry and intergenerational teaching.
- illustration-sheets/sheet-04.png — 12 festival and community scenes: Yangge, Shehuo, lanterns, local opera, market days, seasonal preparation and family memory sharing.
- illustration-sheets/sheet-05.png — 12 kitchen and market scenes: knife-cut noodles, aged vinegar, millet cakes, market produce, family cooking and winter meals.

The fifth sheet supplies the wide home mural. All six images were generated for this prototype and copied into the project. They are editorial scene images, not documentary photographs and must not be presented as evidence of real residents, places or events.

## Original vector scenes

illustrations/scene-01.svg to illustrations/scene-50.svg are locally generated vector scene fallbacks, made with tools/make_illustrations.py. They are original lightweight assets, independent from the generated image sheets.

## First visual direction

visual-draft.png is a previous concept image from before the scope was changed to all of Shanxi. It is not used by the current site. The original village-mural.png has since been replaced with the generated Shanxi loess-village panorama.

## Usage boundary

All illustrative figures, settings and scenes are generated examples. Confirm local architecture, craft details, event context, individual likeness permissions and story provenance with local collaborators before presenting any generated scene as a depiction of a named village.
